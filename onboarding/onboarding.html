SeenDisJob watches while you search

It quietly remembers job postings you visit and warns you when the same opportunity appears again across different sites or tabs.

How it works

1
Browse jobs normally

SeenDisJob reads the visible job title and company name from supported job pages.

2
Duplicates are flagged

If a repost or agency copy matches a job you already saw, a small warning appears in the corner.

3
Applications are counted automatically

LinkedIn Easy Apply is counted separately; applications that open an external form count as Advanced Apply.

4
Review everything from the dashboard

See tracked postings, duplicate history, application totals, and trusted sites from the toolbar popup.

Nothing leaves this device — no server, no account, no analytics

All good.

The extension stays fully inactive — it won't read any page content or watch for job postings.

One more step

Chrome requires this toggle to be flipped by hand — no extension can enable it for you.
