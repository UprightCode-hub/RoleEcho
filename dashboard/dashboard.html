Privacy & permissions

RoleEcho reads job titles and company names from the job pages you visit while it's active.

  • Everything it collects stays in Chrome's local storage on this device.
  • There is no account, no server, no analytics, and no remote job database — nothing is sent anywhere.
  • It only reads page content after you grant content-check consent during setup.
  • Detection also depends on Chrome's "Allow User Scripts" permission being turned on for this extension.
